5TH SEM CORE MODULE

Theory of Computation (TOC)

  • Finite Automata: DFA, NFA, subset construction equivalence, Arden's theorem, state minimization (Hopcroft/table-filling), and Pumping Lemma for Regular Languages.
  • Context-Free Grammars & PDAs: Chomsky Normal Form (CNF), Greibach Normal Form (GNF), Pushdown Automata (final state vs empty stack equivalence), and CFL Pumping Lemma.

  • Undecidability: Halting Problem diagonalization proof (ATM), mapping reducibility, and Rice's Theorem.
  • Complexity Classes: P, NP, Cook-Levin theorem, polynomial-time reductions, and NP-completeness proofs (3-SAT, Vertex Cover, TSP).

Applied Sciences & Security Foundations

  • Physical Sciences: Semiconductor physics, Fermi levels, P-N junctions, quantum tunneling breakdown in sub-3nm MOSFETs, and photonics.
  • Mechanical Systems: High-density thermal dissipation, active/passive cooling, forward/inverse robot kinematics, and mechatronic PWM actuation.
  • Cyber Security Principles: Symmetric (AES) & asymmetric (RSA/ECC) cryptography, PKI, X.509, SHA-256 digests, and STRIDE threat modeling.
  • System Defense: OWASP Top 10 mitigation, memory corruption prevention (buffer overflow/ROP), and zero-trust architecture.
